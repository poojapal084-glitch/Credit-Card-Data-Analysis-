# Credit-Card-Data-Analysis-
Performed Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on credit card customer acquisition, spending, and repayment datasets to identify customer spending patterns and business trends.
Cleaned and preprocessed data by handling missing values, correcting invalid age records, and applying credit-limit-based corrections to spending and repayment transactions.
Used Pandas and NumPy for data manipulation, merging multiple datasets, aggregation, and statistical analysis.
Analyzed monthly spending, repayment trends, bank interest earnings, and profitability using Python.
Identified top spending categories, highest-spending cities, customer age groups, and top 10 customers based on repayment amounts.
Developed visualizations using Matplotlib and Seaborn to analyze city-wise, yearly, and monthly spending patterns and identify potential seasonality.
Created reusable Python functions to identify the top 10 customers by repayment across cities, card categories (Gold, Silver, Platinum), and time periods.
